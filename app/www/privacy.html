RosterOptimizer

Privacy policy

Effective 2 November 2026.

RosterOptimizer suggests weekly fantasy football moves for your team. It is a personal project, not affiliated with ESPN or Yahoo. It has no accounts of its own, no ads and no tracking, and it does not sell or share your data.

What stays on your device

Your league ID, team choice, settings and the roster data the app downloads are saved in the app's own storage on your phone (or in your browser on the website). They never leave your device except as described below. Uninstalling the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, deletes them.

ESPN sign-in

For a private ESPN league, you sign in on ESPN's own page. The app keeps the sign-in cookies ESPN gives back on your device and sends them only to ESPN, to read your league. RosterOptimizer never sees your ESPN password. Signing out in the app deletes the cookies.

Yahoo sign-in (website only)

On the website you can sign in with Yahoo. Yahoo's access tokens are kept in secure cookies in your browser. The site's server passes your requests on to Yahoo and passes the answers back; it does not store or log your league data.

Website traffic

The website is hosted on Cloudflare, which keeps standard, anonymous visit counts (for example, the number of page views). No cookies are used for this.

Children

The app is meant for people aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/notalegacybug/fantasyfootball/issues.
